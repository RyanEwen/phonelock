## Full description

PhoneLock turns your child's Android phone or tablet into a simple, safe home screen with only the apps you choose, and lets you manage it from your own phone.

**Setup:** you set up your child's device from this app over Wi-Fi, with Android's Wireless debugging: no computer, no cable, nothing erased. Their device needs Android 13 or later, and no accounts signed in during setup (you add them back afterwards).

What you can do:
• Choose which apps are on their home screen, and when: normal hours, limited hours and bedtime, on a weekly schedule
• Answer their requests for an app, and allow or pause apps for a while
• See where their device is, and where it has been over the last 7 days
• See its battery level, and get an alert when it runs low or turns off
• Limit calls to a safe list of numbers, and review the call log
• Manage contacts and files, photos and videos
• Ring it at full volume to find it, and view its screen
• Share the device with another parent

Private by design:
• Everything is end-to-end encrypted between your phone and your child's device. Our relay server can't read it.
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts.

Honest with your child:
• Their home screen says that location, battery, calls and app use are shared with you.
• They're notified every time a parent views their screen.
• PhoneLock can't be hidden from them.
